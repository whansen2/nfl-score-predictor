Example Key:
Model -0.5 means the model thinks the favored team should be favored by 0.5 points MORE than the betting line suggests
Model +4.5 means the model thinks the favored team should be favored by 3.5 points LESS than the betting line suggests
Model -2.5 means the model thinks the favored team should be favored by 2.5 points MORE than the betting line suggests
Model -4.5 means the model thinks the favored team should be favored by 4.5 points MORE than the betting line suggests
Model +1.5 means the model thinks the favored team should be favored by 1.5 points LESS than the betting line suggests

For the Eagles example:
Line: Eagles -7.5 (Eagles favored by 7.5)
Model: Eagles win by 8 (Eagles favored by 8)
Model Vs Line: -0.5 means the model is 0.5 points more bullish on the Eagles than the betting line
